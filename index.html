About

A 3-time Kenya Cup champion and 3-time Enterprise Cup champion with Kabras RFC, and top point scorer in the Kenya Cup for the 2025/2026 season. Competing at the top level of Kenyan club rugby while balancing the discipline it takes to stay there season after season.

Off the pitch, a biochemist working as a process manager at a sugar company — bringing the same structure and consistency from the lab and the plant floor into training, mentorship, and building a platform that shares that journey.

The Journey So Far

2018

Fresh Out of High School

Started rugby straight out of high school with no shortcuts and no head start — just a decision to begin. Every player you'll ever admire started exactly here.

2018 – 2022

Built From Scratch at Masinde Muliro University

Joined Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology to study Biochemistry, and joined the university rugby team from the bottom of the depth chart. Worked up through the ranks year by year — no fast-tracking, just consistent effort — until finally earning a place in the main team in the 3rd and 4th years.

Post-Graduation

Arriving at Kabras — A New, Harder Grind

Graduated and moved on to Kabras RFC, stepping into a dressing room full of seasoned players, several with national team caps. Instead of being intimidated by the level, treated it as the next chapter to grind through — because every level up demands starting the work over again.

Season 1

Paying Dues in the Second Team

Spent the first season with Kabras in the second team, earning only cameo appearances in the first team. Stayed patient and kept working — proof that a slow start doesn't define the ceiling.

Seasons 2 & 3

Earned the Starting Jersey

Sealed a starting position by the second season and held it into the third — turning consistent, unglamorous work into a permanent place in the first team.

2025/2026

League Top Point Scorer — 133 Points

Started all 13 of 14 games played and finished the season as the league's top point scorer with 133 points. From second-team cameos to the top of the scoring charts — the throughline the whole way was showing up and doing the work when no one was watching.
